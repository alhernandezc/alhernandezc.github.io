Gender gaps in the Mexican labor market

ENOE microdata · 2005–present · participation, pay, informality

Two decades of Mexico's quarterly labor-force survey (ENOE, INEGI), harmonized into gender-gap indicators by state and quarter: labor-force participation, unemployment, informality, underemployment, unpaid domestic work as a reason for being out of the labor force, and the gender gap in real hourly earnings, all of it with survey-design-based standard errors, and an interactive dashboard by state.

Public-sector vacancies in Mexico

INEGI government censuses · federal budget staffing annex · DOF postings

How many federal public-sector positions sit empty, and where? A public panel integrating three official sources that had not been joined before: INEGI's censuses of government (occupied and vacant positions by institution and rank), the federal budget's staffing annex, and civil-service openings published in the official gazette, collected daily with a verified backfill to 2013. A Mexican counterpart to the California civil-service vacancies study.

Sex composition of Mexican migration & remittances

Banxico remittance corridors · ACS/IPUMS · extends the 2019 JERP paper

Which US states send remittances to Mexico, and how does that relate to the sex composition of the Mexican-born population in each? A corridor panel joining Banco de México remittance flows (by US state of origin since 2013) with the demographic composition of migrant communities. It extends my peer-reviewed work on changing sex ratios in US immigrant communities.

MethodHow these projects work

Each project follows the same rules: public data sources only, no microdata ever redistributed, methodology documented and versioned, and figures that always cite their source. Code is released under MIT; derived data tables under CC BY 4.0.
